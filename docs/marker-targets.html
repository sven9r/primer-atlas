Marker × organism routing

A locus is not a biological target.

One locus can support several organism groups, and one organism group can be investigated with several complementary loci. These links describe established use—not uniform primer coverage.

🐟 🐦

Mitochondrial 12S

Predominantly vertebrate eDNA. MiFish and Teleo are fish-oriented; MiBird and BirT are bird-oriented; 12S-V5 is broader across vertebrates.

🦠

Two different 16S systems

Prokaryotic 16S serves bacteria and Archaea. Animal mitochondrial 16S serves multiple metazoan groups. They never share a reference layer merely because both are called 16S.

🍄 🌊 🧫

28S LSU

Fungal LSU profiling, phytoplankton, dinoflagellates, protists, and broader eukaryotic work require separate primer-level target evidence.

🕷️ 🐾

COI

Strong animal and arthropod resolution, with primer- and lineage-specific bias. It can complement fish or zooplankton surveys where references are suitable.

🧫 🌊

18S

Broad eukaryotes, microbial eukaryotes, protists, plankton, and complementary fungal or metazoan evidence. V4 and V9 are not interchangeable observation processes.

🍄

Fungal ITS

The primary fungal barcode family in the current pilot, with ITS1 and ITS2 assays kept distinct from LSU phylogenetic profiling.
