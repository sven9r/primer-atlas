## Published changes

- Persistent marker selectors restore the arthropod COI map after switching
  through fungal ITS or fungal 18S. Arthropods selects 20 scoped COI pairs.
- Application, environment, and design filters use explicit catalog records.
  Clearing an incompatible filter restores newly eligible pairs. Changing map
  sort preserves manual deselections.
- Dietary metabarcoding selects four published-use pairs: ANML, FWH1, ZBJ_ART,
  and NOSPID (Spidprey). Predator, sample material, amplified role, study source,
  and limitations remain separate fields. Tested variants and component-only
  hybrids do not inherit direct dietary-use evidence.
- Grouped sources omit the laboratory intake sheet. A download generated from
  the curated catalog contains every currently mapped pair, sequences,
  coordinates, application/organism evidence, dietary context, claims, and
  source URLs. PR2 reported amplicon lengths are retained. Structured dietary
  records preserve associations when a pair gains multiple studies.
- Task-oriented navigation, a readiness panel, and map temperature details are
  included. App version 2026.10.02 is shown on Start here.
- The Connect manifest includes the new metadata catalogs and excludes regional
  and fungal research outputs. CI checks required files and their checksums.
